# Annotated Example: Full DM Sale (Inbound to Contract)

A real conversation, anonymized and with identifying details altered. The lead is a B2B consultancy founder (referred by a mutual connection) who submitted an inquiry form. Watch how every message ticks a qualification box, and how price only appears after the summary.

## Phase 1: Confirm and qualify (don't book the call yet)

**Coach:** Hey [name], just got your form submission, thanks! Let's chat :)

**Lead:** Sounds good, let me know if you have a diary link or I can share mine.

**Coach:** I'd love to ask you a few questions just to see if I can help you before we book time.

→ *Key move: the lead wants to skip to a call. The coach slows it down. Qualify first, book later. This protects your calendar and raises your authority.*

**Coach:** Can you tell me more about your offer, as in duration, ICP in a nutshell, avg LTV?

**Lead:** [Explains: one-off and retainer services for corporate clients, LTV from ~10k one-off to ~5k/month retainers]

## Phase 2: Map the funnel with numbers

**Coach:** How many leads p/w are you getting from LinkedIn right now? And what would be the goal?

**Lead:** Maybe 1 every 3-6 months. Goal would be a regular monthly flow, maybe 1 per week.

**Coach:** Got it, so you're getting leads through referrals right now, correct? May I ask about your current MRR? And what it ideally would be in 3-6 months?

→ *Situation, gap, and goal, all in numbers. These numbers come back later in the math.*

**Coach:** I just went through your website and as far as I can tell there's 1 case study. Do you have 3-5 case studies with concrete metrics that you could share in content?

→ *Named preparation: "I just went through your website." Proves you looked. Also surfaces a proof gap you'll work on together.*

## Phase 3: Affirm and dig into history

**Coach:** Ok this all sounds great. I see you worked at [well-known firm] and have been building your business for years so you must have tons of stories we could share. You also have a few thousand followers which is a great starting point.

**Coach:** I'm curious: what did your 3 ghostwriters write about if you'd have to summarize it? Just to clarify: I'm not a ghostwriter but I coach people to get more business out of LinkedIn.

→ *Two things: (1) asks what they already tried, (2) positions against the failed alternative without trashing it.*

## Phase 4: Transparency + capacity check

**Coach:** First I'll be transparent with you: we're expecting our 2nd daughter end of July so I'll be offline for a few weeks. I pause collaborations in August anyway. Just mentioning this in case you're in a hurry.

→ *Real constraints shared proactively. Builds trust and creates a natural timeline.*

**Coach:** So 1 lead weekly is realistic and arguably conservative. We'll have to post and engage 5 times per week to get to that stage. Would you have the capacity for this?

**Lead:** I would have to make capacity for it. I have an exec assistant if anything can be delegated.

→ *Capacity objection surfaces early, before the pitch. The coach solves it (assistant joins sessions, gets resources) instead of ignoring it.*

## Phase 5: Summary + pitch (still no price)

**Coach:** Considering everything we've chatted about, I can help you. Here's what I have in mind:
1. We improve your positioning so the right people on LinkedIn resonate with it
2. I review your bestselling service and we'll (re)package it if needed
3. I help you create content, sell in the DMs, and close in calls

→ *The summary-pitch. Three pillars, mapped to what the lead said. Boxes 1-3 ticked.*

## Phase 6: The math, with their numbers

**Coach:** Quick math:
Conservative: 3 clients → 15k MRR → 90k LTV
Ideally: 6 clients → 30k MRR → 180k LTV
Without the 10k offer.
You're the numbers expert among us so feel free to correct me if I'm off.

→ *Their numbers, calculated conservatively, with a disarming line at the end. The upside now anchors the price.*

**Lead:** Sounds good. I would like to scale to 50k MRR soon enough.

**Coach:** One of my clients grew from 15k to 40-50k MRR since September. She is in a different space but just to show you it is possible.

→ *Similar-client proof, real numbers, honest caveat ("different space").*

## Phase 7: Structure, then price

**Coach:** I'd work with you 1:1 for 6 months through biweekly calls and async reviews on weekdays. We'd pause in August and pick up in September, which is generally a good month for conversion. If you like this, I can share the investment with you too.

**Lead:** Sounds good, please share.

**Coach:** Glad to hear it sounds good. The investment for everything mentioned above is 16.5k EUR.

→ *One sentence. No defense, no apology. Note: he asked permission to share the investment first. Another opt-in.*

**Lead:** What are your payment terms?

**Coach:** 16.5k is paid in full with 1.5k EUR off. A payment plan would be optional and that would be 3k monthly.

→ *Upfront gets a discount. The plan doesn't. The amount never drops.*

## Phase 8: Objections after the deck

The lead reviews the deck, then comes back with real objections. Watch each response:

**Lead:** Ultimately I'm after more leads, not impressions, not engagement. How has your process worked for lead generation? My content has struggled to pull in leads. I also get the wrong audience engaging (aspiring juniors in regions where my clients aren't).

**Coach:** I don't care about views or likes. To be frank, they can actually go down because I focus on lead gen metrics and content like that isn't for everyone. Have you reviewed my deck? There's plenty of screenshots regarding leads and closed deals. Also on my profile's recommendation section you can find plenty (works better than just me tooting my own horn).

→ *Agrees with the premise, redirects to proof that already exists. Doesn't get defensive.*

**Lead:** I'd be most interested in working on a monthly basis. And I might not be able to commit 1.5 hours daily.

**Coach:** Just to clarify, it takes time to create and improve content and turn conversations with your ICP into business, so I don't offer a month-to-month option. As for your capacity, how much LinkedIn-related work could your assistant take off your plate?

→ *Holds the line on structure, solves the capacity problem. Never both concede.*

## Phase 9: Close

**Coach:** If that all sounds good, let's conclude things and I'll share a contract with you asap so we can potentially kick off this week.

→ *Clear, low-pressure close with a concrete next step and momentum.*

**Lead:** Would be good to see a contract as a next step.

## Follow-up cadence used in this conversation

After sending the deck by email, the coach followed up:
- Next morning: "Hey [name], email sent. Did you get it?"
- Two days later: "Hey [name], are you in? 💪"

Short, light, zero desperation. The lead came back with real questions, which is exactly what follow-ups are for: surfacing the objections you can then resolve.
