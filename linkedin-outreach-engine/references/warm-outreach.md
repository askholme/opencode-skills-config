# Warm Outreach Scripts

Match the trigger to the script. All follow the same logic: connect, open with their action, qualify with one question at a time, bridge to a 20-minute call. Once they reply and a conversation runs, the linkedin-sales-sparring skill takes over.

## Trigger: they liked or commented on your post

Connection request, then:

> Hey [name], thanks for your comment/like on my post about [topic]. Why did it resonate with you?

Or:

> Hey [name], thanks for interacting with my post about [topic]. What topic about [your solution] would you like to read more of in my content?

Then one question per message: is this something you're dealing with now → when did you realize → what have you tried → what's the goal. Then bridge:

> I recently helped [similar ICP] with [problem they mentioned]. They [result]. Would you like to know more about it?

Yes → 20-min call plus scheduling link. No → lead magnet, follow up after 2 weeks.

## Trigger: they viewed your profile

Connection request:

> Hey [name], I noticed [common interest on their profile or content]. Happy to connect with you.

After accepting (also when they don't reply):

> Thanks for connecting. Are you interested in [your content pillar/offer] or were you just looking around?

Problem surfaces → similar-client story → short call. Just looking → ask how they currently handle [your domain], listen for pain.

## Trigger: they started following you

Connection request: "Thanks for the follow, [name]! I noticed [common interest or something from their profile]." After accepting:

> Are you interested in [your content pillar/offer] or did my content just cross your feed?

Then the same fork as profile views: problem surfaces → similar-client story → short call. Just browsing → ask about how they handle [your domain] today.

## Trigger: they sent you a connection request

Accept, then:

> Hey [name], thanks for connecting. Are you interested in my [solution] services or are you just expanding your network?

Interested → similar-client story → "does this sound like something you would need?"
Just networking → "what topic would you like to learn more about in my content?" → listen for pain → bridge.

Generic or automated-looking message? One gentle nudge after 2 days, nothing more.

## Trigger: they asked the price right away

Never a naked number this early:

> I would need a bit more information to answer that question. Shall we schedule a 20-min call?

For the full price-in-DM structure, the linkedin-sales-sparring skill has the play.

## Follow-up cadence

**Standard leads:**
- Day 2: voice note, or "Thoughts?" / "[name]?"
- Day 4: "Anything back on this?"
- Day 6: "I know, LinkedIn messaging is not the best. How about we just jump on a 20-min call to talk about solving [problem]?" plus scheduling link
- Then: dead lead. NEXT.

**Warm leads who showed real interest, then went quiet:** lower the friction instead of nudging harder:
- Attempt 1: "To make it easier, here's my meeting link."
- Attempt 2: "Is this still relevant for you?"
- Every 2 business days, 3-4 attempts max.

Most deals close in follow-ups. Detach from the emotional outcome; people are busy.
