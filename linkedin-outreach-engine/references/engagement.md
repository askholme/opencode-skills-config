# Engagement & Commenting

Commenting is outreach in public. It warms up prospects before you ever DM them, feeds the algorithm, and makes your name familiar in your ICP's world. The earned-pitch play in cold-outreach.md literally requires it.

## Who to comment on

Build two lists:

1. **ICPs who post** — your future clients. Commenting here is pre-outreach: when you DM them later, you're not a stranger
2. **Peers and big accounts in your niche (30k+ followers)** — their audience contains your ICP. Smart comments here are visibility with borrowed reach

Use Sales Navigator or a simple list of 15-25 accounts. Check the "active in last 30 days" signal; commenting on ghosts is wasted effort.

## What makes a comment work

- Add something: an experience, a counterpoint, a sharper example, a question that deepens the thread. "Great post!" is invisible
- 2-4 sentences. Long enough to show thinking, short enough to get read
- Write for the author's audience, not only the author. Your comment is content
- Be early. The first hour of a post is where comments get seen
- Disagree when you disagree, kindly. A grounded counterpoint gets more profile visits than agreement

## Daily target

5-10 comments per day, part of the daily routine (see daily-routine.md). Comment BEFORE you post your own content; it warms up your reach.

## From comment to conversation

Watch what happens after you comment:

- Author or others reply to your comment → keep the thread going, then move to DM if there's ICP signal: "Enjoyed this thread. [Question about their situation]?"
- Someone likes your comment and fits your ICP → profile view them, they often view back, now they're a warm profile-view lead (see warm-outreach.md)
- You've commented on someone's posts 3+ times over 2 weeks → you've earned a warm opener: "Hey [name], I keep running into your posts about [topic]. [Specific question or observation]."

Engagement without conversion attempts is a hobby. Every week, some comments should turn into DMs.
