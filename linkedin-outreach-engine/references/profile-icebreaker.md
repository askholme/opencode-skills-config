# Profile to Icebreaker

A step plan for turning one LinkedIn profile into one personal opening line. Works two ways:

- **With Claude in Chrome:** the user opens the profile in their browser and asks Claude to scan it. Claude reads the page directly.
- **Without Chrome:** the user pastes the profile (headline, about, recent activity) and ideally their 2-3 most recent posts.

**Hard rule: Claude drafts, the human sends.** Never automate sending, connection requests, or any LinkedIn action. LinkedIn bans accounts for automation, and a banned account kills the user's entire channel. Reading and drafting with a human in the loop is the line; stay behind it.

## Step 1: Scan for signals (in this order of value)

1. **Recent posts** — what are they writing about, and what's their angle or opinion? A post from the last 2 weeks is the best icebreaker material that exists
2. **Recent activity** — what did they comment on or reshare? Shows what's on their mind even if they don't post
3. **About section** — their own words for what they do and care about; mine their phrasing
4. **Headline and role** — context, rarely icebreaker material by itself
5. **Common ground** — shared connections, groups, location, background, interests
6. **Company signals** — hiring, launches, news (for the earned-pitch play in cold-outreach.md)

## Step 2: Pick ONE signal

One specific detail beats three generic ones. Prefer, in order: a recent post with an opinion > recent activity > a specific About phrase > common ground. Never use: profile photo compliments, "impressive background", or anything that could be said to 100 other people.

## Step 3: Draft the one-liner

Formats that work:

- **Post reaction:** "Your post about [topic] stuck with me, especially [their specific point]. [Genuine question or your take in one line]?"
- **Activity observation:** "I keep seeing you in the comments on [topic]. Curious: [question about their view]?"
- **Common ground:** "Fellow [shared thing] here. I noticed [specific detail]. Happy to connect."
- **About echo:** "You wrote '[their exact phrase]' in your About. That's rare to see said out loud. [One-line why it resonates]."

Rules:
- 1-2 sentences maximum
- Their words or their content, not yours
- No pitch, no offer, no "I help X do Y" in the icebreaker
- End with a question when it fits naturally; don't force one
- The test: could this line be sent to anyone else? If yes, rewrite

## Step 4: Route it

- Sending a connection request → the one-liner is the note (or shorter)
- Already connected → the one-liner opens the DM, then follow the warm-outreach.md flow
- Senior cold target → the icebreaker is step 1 of the earned pitch (cold-outreach.md); the profile scan doubles as your research

## Step 5: Log it

Add the person to the tracker with source, date, and the signal used. The signal matters later: "you mentioned X three weeks ago" is a follow-up gift.

## Batch mode

For a list session (e.g. 10 profiles in 30 minutes): scan, draft, and log per profile in one pass. Quality bar stays the same; if a profile yields no real signal, skip the person rather than sending something generic. A smaller list of real icebreakers beats a full list of noise.
